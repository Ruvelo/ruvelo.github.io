Built the same way, every time

Every Ruvelo package follows one standard, so once you've used one, you know them all.

  • Installs in one commandNo build step, no extra services. It uses your database and your logins.
  • Looks finishedA real UI in light and dark, readable on a phone, and easy to restyle or drop into your layout.
  • Made for developersTyped PHP and JSON APIs, events, factories, and guidelines for AI coding agents.
  • Kept healthyStrict types, PHPStan level 8 and tests on every Laravel and PHP version we support.
